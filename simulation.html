REGGE TRAJECTORIES — spin grows linearly with mass²: the signature of a string. Open: J = α′M² + 1. Closed: J = α′M²/2 + 2 (slope halves, intercept doubles). Dots are allowed states (integer levels n).
Hadrons (ρ, a₂, ρ₃…) lie on near-linear Regge trajectories with α′ ≈ 0.9 GeV⁻² — the historical origin of string theory (Veneziano 1968).

VENEZIANO AMPLITUDE — the 1968 formula that started string theory: A(s,t) = Γ(−α(s)) Γ(−α(t)) / Γ(−α(s)−α(t)). Every pole (dashed line) is a particle of the Regge tower, at α(s) = n. Slide α(t) to see the shape change.
α(t) = -1.50
Veneziano found it by guessing a function with infinitely many poles; Nambu, Nielsen and Susskind then showed it describes vibrating strings. Real hadron data follow it only roughly — which is why strings were abandoned for QCD, then revived as a theory of gravity.
HODGE DIAMOND — the topology of a Calabi–Yau 3-fold in one picture. h¹¹ (amber) counts Kähler (size) moduli and h²¹ (orange) complex-structure (shape) moduli. Pick a manifold, then flip the mirror.
A SHORT HISTORY OF STRING THEORY
1968 — Veneziano writes down the amplitude that fits hadron scattering.
1970 — Nambu, Nielsen and Susskind: the amplitude describes vibrating strings.
1974 — Scherk & Schwarz: the massless spin-2 mode is the graviton — strings as quantum gravity.
1984 — Green & Schwarz anomaly cancellation (SO(32), E₈×E₈): the first superstring revolution.
1985 — Heterotic string; Candelas–Horowitz–Strominger–Witten compactify on Calabi–Yau spaces.
1991 — Candelas et al. discover mirror symmetry on the quintic.
1995 — Witten proposes M-theory; Polchinski identifies D-branes: the second revolution.
1996 — Strominger & Vafa count black-hole microstates and reproduce S = A/4.
1997 — Maldacena: AdS/CFT, a holographic duality between gravity and gauge theory.
2000–03 — Bousso–Polchinski flux landscape; KKLT de Sitter vacua; the landscape debate begins.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM
Leading-order relations only. Nobody can yet derive the Standard Model's masses from a compactification, and the swampland programme questions whether many such vacua are consistent at all. O(1) factors in MPl and αGUT differ between conventions, so they are written “∼”, with no false precision. Λ here comes from a 3-flux toy lattice. In the particle views only the number of families, the gauge couplings (one-loop MSSM running) and the Planck scale are computed from the model; individual particle masses of the modified universe are not derived and are drawn as SCHEMATIC fuzzy clouds.
